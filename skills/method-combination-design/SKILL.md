# Method combination design

Use at least two method cards. Specify component order, information flow, objective changes,
conflicts and their resolution. Cite the Evidence IDs that establish source mechanisms, while
labeling the combination itself as a hypothesis. Provide assumptions, novelty risk and at least
one experiment that could disprove the expected benefit. Return no more than three candidates.
