# Method transfer analysis

Map mechanism to a concrete target pain point. Choose `adapt`, `combine`, or `reject`; rejection
is a useful result. Separate cited paper facts from the transfer hypothesis. Check representation,
objective, data/environment, compute and algorithmic assumptions. Always state conditions, risks,
confidence, and a falsification test. Never claim that a proposed transfer is novel.
